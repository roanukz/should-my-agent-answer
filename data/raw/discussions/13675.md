## Accepted answer by luzzodev (CONTRIBUTOR)

Hello @Zhoros, 
when you return directly an instance of `Response `, in your case `PlainTextResponse`, this is the one that will be used and the one declared in dependecy will be ignored. 
To look at it from another perspective: if a `Response` is declared as a dependency, it will be used to construct the final response together with the value returned by the endpoint. If, instead, an instance of Response is returned directly, that will be taken as the final response.

Hope this helps.
