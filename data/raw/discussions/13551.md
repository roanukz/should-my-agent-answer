## Accepted answer by luzzodev (CONTRIBUTOR)

Hello @jbmoorhouse,
Thanks for submitting your proposal.
While I understand your suggestion, I personally don't like it. There are already several ways to handle custom encoding, and since Version is just a common object, I believe the correct JSON representation is the current one, not its string counterpart.
