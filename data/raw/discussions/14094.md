## Accepted answer by luzzodev (CONTRIBUTOR)

Hi @GniLudio, `fastapi dev` and `fastapi run` are both thin wrappers around the same internal `_run()` function. They differ only in their default options:

**fastapi dev**: `enables` --reload and binds to 127.0.0.1.
**fastapi run**: `disables` --reload and binds to 0.0.0.0.

All other options are shared; you can override the defaults (e.g., --host, --reload) with flags on either command. The differences are also documented in the FastAPI CLI README: https://github.com/fastapi/fastapi-cli
