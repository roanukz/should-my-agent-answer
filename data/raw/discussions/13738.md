app = fastapi.FastAPI()

    request: fastapi.Request,
):
    async def _stream():
        body = await request.body()

async def upper_annotated(
    body: Annotated[bytes, fastapi.Body()],
):
    async def _stream():
        yield body

    return StreamingResponse(process_stream(_stream()))

## Accepted answer by luzzodev (CONTRIBUTOR)

Hello @chamini2,
All three solutions are valid and effectively equivalent. The issue is that in the first two cases you’re attempting to read the stream after the response has started, whereas in the third case the stream and body are read (and cached) by the request before the response. In your specific scenario, the first two streams get consumed by the “listen for disconnect” handler. If you read the body (or the stream) before sending the response, you won’t run into any problems.
