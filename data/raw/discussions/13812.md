## Accepted answer by YuriiMotov (MEMBER)

In this case `c: int` is `Query` parameter, not `Body`.

You need to annotate `c` with `Body()` to turn it into `Body` parameter:

```python
@app.post("/test")
async def test(a: A, c: Annotated[int, Body()]):
  return {"a":a, "c":c}
```
