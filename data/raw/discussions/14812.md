### Description

I upgraded my code to use `status.HTTP_422_UNPROCESSABLE_CONTENT` when I was first hit with AttributeError for `status.HTTP_422_UNPROCESSABLE_ENTITY`.

I had checked the starlette documentation at that time and I expected FastAPI to pin lower limit of starlette to prevent breaking changes downstream. I got a failure on a machine where starlette 0.47.2 was being used and later found out that it was decided to not to pin the lower limit in https://github.com/fastapi/fastapi/pull/14077.

I have resolved the issue at my end, but I did expect FastAPI to pin the lower limit and not cause issues downstream 😅

## Accepted answer by YuriiMotov (MEMBER)

FastAPI doesn't use neither `status.HTTP_422_UNPROCESSABLE_ENTITY` nor `status.HTTP_422_UNPROCESSABLE_CONTENT` internally. So, there is no incompatibility issue on FastAPI's side.

I think you should fix this on your side by pinning the version of Starlette in your application's dependencies
