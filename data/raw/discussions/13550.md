## Accepted answer by luzzodev (CONTRIBUTOR)

Hello @charleeBlaqTech,

Your Fastapi version is quite old, you are trying to import a function `_merge_lifespan_context` that is not defined.
Probably it has been added in later version. 
Anyway I personally discourage the usage of private functions, since they can change over time and are not officially exposed.
