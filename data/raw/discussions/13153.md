## Accepted answer by haithcockce (NONE)

I am working to follow various tutorials. Unfortunately, there seems to be roughly a million out there with slightly different ways of doing it. 

Coming back to it today, I don't think I actually need the `return {"request": request}` at all in any of these points, especially since this is not the `/api` part of the app and rather just need to return the form again. As such, replacing that line with `return templates.TemplateResponse("login.html", context={"request": request})` fixes it and actually has the app do what I want it to do. 

Thank you!
