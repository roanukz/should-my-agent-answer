### Additional Context

I am encountering a persistent deployment failure when executing the `fastapi deploy` command within environments managed by `uv`. The issue appears specifically linked to the presence of `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock` files. Conversely, deployments utilizing standard `pip` and `requirements.txt` proceed without incident, suggesting a compatibility gap in how the deployment tool handles `uv`'s lockfile or binary management.

Current State & Observations
- Trigger: The error manifests exclusively when `uv` is used for dependency management (source tree contains `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock`).
- Baseline: Deployments are successful when falling back to `pip` and `requirements.txt` manifests.
- Remediation Attempted: Following community discussions (Ref: [FastAPI Discussion #15278](https://github.com/fastapi/fastapi/discussions/15278)), I have upgraded fastapi-cloud-cli to the latest version. However, this has not resolved the underlying conflict.
